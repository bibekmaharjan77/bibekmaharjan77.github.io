Hi, I am
Bibek Maharjan A PhD Student A Teaching Assistant

I’m a PhD student and Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Scalable Computer Architecture & Emerging Technologies Laboratory (SCALE) at Kent State University’s Department of Computer Science. I joined the lab in August 2023 and work under Dr. Gokarna Sharma, contributing to innovative research in parallel and distributed computing.

I’m interested in how computers in a network make decisions with only a partial view of what comes next. In my research, I asked a concrete question. If we can predict which computer will next need a shared object/service, how much communication can we save when that prediction is right, and what does it cost when we guess wrong?

I’m also drawn to routing problems where a decision made now affects how quickly later requests can be served. For that, I am studying online routing with single or multiple repairpersons on a line. In this problem, each new request raises two questions: which repairperson should serve it, and where should that repairperson go next? Read more about my research interests.

Before joining Kent State, I worked as a Software Developer at Outlines Research and Development and as a Data Specialist at Cloudfactory Nepal (a subsidiary of Cloudfactory LLC, USA).
